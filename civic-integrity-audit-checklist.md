CIVIC INTEGRITY DIVISION — AUDIT CHECKLIST
This checklist defines the mandatory audit steps for evaluating municipal integrity, constitutional compliance, ethical alignment, and operational transparency. It is used during scheduled audits, unannounced inspections, misconduct investigations, and pattern‑of‑practice reviews.

I. Constitutional & Legal Compliance
Constitutional Alignment Review — Verify all municipal actions comply with constitutional rights.

Authority Boundary Verification — Confirm actors operate within lawful jurisdiction.

Due Process Compliance — Ensure procedures protect civilian rights.

Statutory Requirements Check — Validate adherence to state and municipal law.

II. Ethical Governance & Conduct
Ethical Standards Assessment — Evaluate behavior against DAO ethical doctrine.

Conflict of Interest Scan — Identify financial, relational, or political conflicts.

Misconduct Indicator Review — Check for signs of corruption or abuse.

Retaliation Risk Evaluation — Ensure no retaliatory actions against civilians or staff.

III. Transparency & Documentation Integrity
Audit Trail Verification — Confirm logs are complete, immutable, and unaltered.

Record Accessibility Check — Ensure CID has full access to required documents.

Incident Documentation Review — Validate accuracy and completeness of incident reports.

Public Transparency Sync — Confirm required data is published to dashboards.

IV. Telemetry & Pattern Analysis
Pattern‑of‑Practice Scan — Identify repeated misconduct or systemic failures.

Risk Indicator Review — Evaluate telemetry‑generated risk scores.

Anomaly Detection Check — Flag irregular municipal behavior.

Integrity Score Validation — Confirm scoring accuracy and kernel alignment.

V. Civilian Protection & Rights Safeguards
Rights Violation Assessment — Investigate any potential rights infringements.

Civilian Complaint Review — Ensure proper intake, handling, and resolution.

Trauma‑Preventive Standards Check — Validate non‑punitive, harm‑reduction practices.

Vulnerable Population Safeguards — Ensure protections for at‑risk groups.

VI. Corrective Action & Enforcement
Corrective Directive Review — Confirm directives were issued when required.

Compliance Monitoring Loop — Verify ongoing adherence to corrective actions.

Escalation Protocol Check — Ensure unresolved violations were escalated properly.

Remediation Effectiveness Evaluation — Assess whether corrective actions resolved issues.
