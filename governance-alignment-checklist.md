GOVERNANCE ALIGNMENT CHECKLIST
The Governance Alignment Checklist (GAC) is the mandatory evaluation tool used by the Civic Integrity Division to verify that all municipal operations adhere to the structural, ethical, constitutional, and documentation standards defined by Beast System 3.0.

The checklist ensures that no municipal action proceeds unless it is fully aligned with governance kernels, rights protections, and audit‑ready documentation.

1. Authority & Identity Alignment
All municipal actions must satisfy:

Authority Verification — Actor identity validated through Identity Kernel

Role & Scope Confirmation — Action falls within lawful authority

Chain‑of‑Command Integrity — No bypassing of required approvals

DAO Anchoring Check — Action aligns with DAO‑anchored governance identity

Failure in this category → Immediate HALT

2. Documentation & Audit Trail Alignment
Documentation must be:

Complete & Accurate — No missing or contradictory entries

Immutable — Append‑only, hash‑anchored

Timestamp‑Verified — Kernel‑generated timestamps only

Chain‑of‑Custody Logged — All access and transfers recorded

Public Transparency Ready — Non‑sensitive data prepared for dashboards

Failure in this category → Escalation to Accountability Division

3. Ethics & Rights Alignment
All actions must pass Ethics Kernel evaluation:

Dignity Preservation — No humiliation, coercion, or dehumanization

Harm Prevention — No avoidable physical or emotional harm

Constitutional Compliance — No rights violations

Non‑Discrimination — No disparate impact

Trauma‑Preventive Engagement — Required for all civilian interactions

Failure in this category → ETHICS HALT

4. Telemetry & Pattern Alignment
Telemetry must confirm:

No Anomalies Detected — No workflow or behavioral irregularities

No Corruption Signatures — No relational or procedural red flags

No Retaliation Indicators — No adverse patterns toward civilians

No Systemic Risk Patterns — No escalating municipal failures

Failure in this category → Telemetry Alert + Integrity Review

5. Operational Alignment
Operational actions must satisfy:

Continuity Requirements — No disruption to essential services

Resource Equity — Fair, transparent distribution

Non‑Punitive Operations — No coercive or punitive logistics

Infrastructure Safety — No risk to public facilities or utilities

Failure in this category → MOSD Corrective Directive

6. Civilian Protection Alignment
Civilians must be protected through:

Retaliation Prohibition — Zero tolerance

Confidentiality Standards — Identity protected

Accessibility Requirements — Disability, language, trauma accommodations

Vulnerable Population Safeguards — Enhanced protections required

Failure in this category → Ombudsman Review + Ethics Escalation

7. Public Transparency Alignment
All actions must support:

Dashboard Sync — Non‑sensitive data published

Public Reporting Standards — Clear, rights‑centered communication

Accountability Documentation — Evidence prepared for public review

Failure in this category → Accountability Division Correction

8. Final Alignment Decision
The Civic Integrity Division issues one of three determinations:

PROCEED — Fully aligned with governance standards

REVIEW — Requires cross‑division evaluation

HALT — Violates governance, ethics, or constitutional safeguards

A HALT cannot be overridden without Ethics Kernel approval.
