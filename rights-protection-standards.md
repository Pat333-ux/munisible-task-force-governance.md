RIGHTS PROTECTION STANDARDS
The Rights Protection Standards (RPS) establish the mandatory, non‑negotiable requirements for safeguarding civilian rights, dignity, autonomy, and constitutional protections across all municipal operations. These standards bind every division, every municipal actor, and every automated system within Beast System 3.0.

The RPS ensures that no municipal action may proceed if it violates dignity, causes preventable harm, or infringes constitutional rights.

1. Mission & Purpose
The Rights Protection Standards exist to:

Protect civilians from harm, coercion, discrimination, and retaliation

Ensure all municipal actions comply with constitutional and ethical safeguards

Provide a unified rights‑centered framework for all divisions

Anchor Beast System 3.0 to immutable human‑dignity principles

Prevent misuse of authority, data, or municipal power

Guarantee transparency, fairness, and trauma‑preventive engagement

These standards form the ethical and constitutional backbone of the Munisible Task Force.

2. Core Rights Protection Principles
2.1 Dignity & Respect
All civilians must be treated with inherent dignity, regardless of circumstance.
Municipal actions must avoid humiliation, shaming, or dehumanization.

2.2 Autonomy & Consent
Civilians must be given choices whenever possible.
Coercion is prohibited except in imminent life‑threatening emergencies.

2.3 Non‑Discrimination
Municipal actions must not discriminate based on protected characteristics or socioeconomic status.
Disparate impact triggers mandatory review.

2.4 Transparency & Explanation
Civilians must be informed of the nature, basis, and implications of municipal actions affecting them.

2.5 Due Process
All civilians must receive fair, rights‑centered procedures, including the opportunity to respond before adverse actions.

2.6 Safety & Trauma Prevention
Municipal engagement must minimize emotional, psychological, and physical harm.
Force is a last‑resort, life‑saving measure only.

3. Mandatory Rights Safeguards
3.1 Constitutional Safeguards
No unlawful detention

No unreasonable search or seizure

No suppression of lawful speech or assembly

No retaliation for complaints or criticism

3.2 Documentation Safeguards
All actions must be documented in immutable audit trails

Missing or altered documentation triggers automatic alerts

Civilians have the right to request documentation related to their case

3.3 Telemetry Safeguards
Telemetry must detect rights‑impact indicators

Retaliation signals trigger immediate Ethics review

Disproportionate impact patterns must be flagged and corrected

3.4 Identity & Data Safeguards
Identity data must not be misused, weaponized, or accessed without lawful basis

Data access must follow constitutional search standards

Sensitive data must be encrypted and access‑controlled

4. Civilian Protection Protocols
4.1 Retaliation Prohibition
Retaliation is a critical violation.
Any retaliation attempt triggers automatic Ethics and Telemetry alerts.

4.2 Accessibility Requirements
Rights protections must be accessible to all civilians, including those with disabilities, language barriers, or trauma‑related needs.

4.3 Confidentiality Standards
Civilians have the right to confidential reporting and protected identity unless disclosure is legally required.

4.4 Vulnerable Population Protections
Enhanced safeguards apply to:

Youth

Individuals with disabilities

Marginalized or historically harmed groups

Individuals in crisis or distress
