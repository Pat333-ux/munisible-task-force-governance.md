MUNICIPAL RESPONSE PROTOCOLS
The Municipal Response Protocols (MRP) establish the unified, rights‑centered, trauma‑preventive, and operationally stable response framework for all municipal actions under Beast System 3.0.
These protocols ensure that every municipal response—routine or emergency—remains ethical, constitutional, and aligned with DAO‑anchored governance.

1. Mission & Purpose
The Municipal Response Protocols exist to:

Ensure consistent, ethical, and non‑punitive municipal responses

Protect civilians during incidents, disruptions, or service failures

Maintain operational continuity and infrastructure stability

Provide clear cross‑division coordination standards

Anchor all responses to constitutional and rights‑protection requirements

Integrate telemetry‑driven risk detection into every response

These protocols form the operational backbone of municipal engagement.

2. Response Activation Framework
Municipal responses activate through one of four triggers:

Telemetry Alerts — anomaly, risk, or rights‑impact detection

Civilian Requests — direct need, complaint, or assistance request

Operational Failures — infrastructure or service degradation

Environmental or Crisis Events — weather, hazards, or public safety threats

Each trigger routes through the MOSD Response Engine.

3. Response Levels
3.1 Level 1 — Routine Response
Used for standard municipal tasks.
Requirements:

Dignity‑centered engagement

Documentation entry

No escalation unless telemetry flags anomalies

3.2 Level 2 — Elevated Response
Used when risk indicators rise above baseline.
Requirements:

Telemetry monitoring

Supervisor notification

Rights‑impact check

3.3 Level 3 — Critical Response
Used when civilian safety, rights, or infrastructure stability is threatened.
Requirements:

Immediate MOSD activation

Ethics Kernel evaluation

Cross‑division coordination

3.4 Level 4 — Emergency Response
Used for imminent threats or system failures.
Requirements:

Continuity plan activation

Emergency logistics deployment

Public communication protocols

4. Civilian Interaction Protocols
