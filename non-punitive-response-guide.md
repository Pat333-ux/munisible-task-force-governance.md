NON‑PUNITIVE RESPONSE GUIDE
The Non‑Punitive Response Guide (NPRG) establishes the mandatory standards for all municipal safety interventions conducted under the Munisible Task Force. It replaces force‑based, coercive, or punitive municipal responses with trauma‑preventive, rights‑centered, civilian‑first engagement.

This guide ensures that every municipal interaction preserves dignity, reduces harm, and aligns with Beast System 3.0 ethics and constitutional safeguards.

I. Core Principles of Non‑Punitive Response
Dignity First — Every civilian is treated with inherent worth, regardless of circumstance.

Safety Without Force — Force is never a default; it is a last‑resort life‑saving measure only.

Trauma‑Preventive Engagement — All actions minimize psychological and emotional harm.

Voluntary Cooperation — Civilians are invited, not compelled, to participate in resolution.

Transparency & Consent — Explain actions, seek consent, and avoid surprises.

These principles anchor the division’s operational identity.

II. Standard Non‑Punitive Response Sequence
1. Stabilize the Environment — Reduce noise, crowds, and sensory overload.

2. Approach Calmly — Slow movement, open posture, non‑threatening tone.

3. Assess Emotional State — Identify fear, panic, trauma triggers, or escalation risk.

4. Engage With Respect — Use soft tone, reflective listening, and non‑judgmental language.

5. Offer Choices — Provide options to restore agency and reduce fear.

6. Support Stabilization — Grounding techniques, breathing guidance, safe‑space creation.

7. Connect to Resources — Mental health, shelter, food, medical, or crisis support.

This sequence is mandatory for all CSTPD personnel.

III. Prohibited Actions
No Coercion — No threats, intimidation, or forced compliance.

No Punitive Measures — No punishment, shaming, or retaliatory behavior.

No Escalation Tactics — No raised voices, aggressive posture, or rapid movement.

No Rights Violations — Constitutional protections remain intact at all times.

These prohibitions are absolute.

IV. Telemetry‑Integrated Safety Logic
Risk Indicator Monitoring — Telemetry identifies emotional and situational risk.

Pattern‑of‑Practice Alerts — Detects repeated municipal harm patterns.

Civilian Safety Scoring — Real‑time safety metrics guide intervention.

Telemetry ensures proactive protection, not reactive force.
