EMERGENCY LOGISTICS GUIDE
The Emergency Logistics Guide (ELG) establishes the mandatory standards for non‑punitive, rights‑centered, trauma‑preventive logistical operations during emergencies. It ensures that all municipal support actions prioritize civilian wellbeing, infrastructure stability, and transparent resource deployment.

The guide is enforced by the Municipal Operations Support Division (MOSD) and integrates with the Telemetry, Ethics, and Accountability kernels.

1. Mission & Purpose
The Emergency Logistics Guide exists to:

Provide rapid, ethical, non‑punitive logistical support

Maintain municipal stability during crises or disruptions

Ensure equitable and transparent resource distribution

Protect vulnerable populations through prioritized support

Coordinate cross‑division emergency operations

Prevent systemic failures using telemetry‑driven early warnings

MOSD is the logistical lifeline of the Munisible Task Force.

2. Emergency Activation Criteria
Emergency logistics protocols activate when:

Telemetry detects infrastructure degradation

Critical municipal systems show failure indicators

Environmental or weather events threaten civilian safety

Resource shortages impact vulnerable populations

Documentation anomalies indicate operational instability

The Executive Authority or Ethics Kernel issues a halt or review directive

Activation is automatic when thresholds are met.

3. Core Emergency Logistics Functions
3.1 Civilian‑Centered Resource Deployment
Prioritize vulnerable populations

Deploy food, water, shelter, and medical support

Avoid coercive or punitive distribution practices

Maintain transparent allocation logs

3.2 Infrastructure Stabilization Support
Reinforce utilities, transportation, and public facilities

Activate redundancy systems

Coordinate with Telemetry for real‑time risk detection

3.3 Cross‑Division Emergency Coordination
Work with Community Safety for non‑punitive field support

Provide data to Telemetry for risk scoring

Sync documentation with Accountability Division

3.4 Continuity of Operations
Maintain essential municipal services

Implement continuity plans for critical systems

Prevent cascading failures using predictive modeling

4. Resource Allocation Protocols
Equitable Distribution Standard
Resources must be allocated based on:

Severity of need

Vulnerability status

Rights‑centered criteria

Telemetry‑verified risk indicators

Anti‑Corruption Safeguards
All allocations must be logged

Telemetry monitors for irregular patterns

Unauthorized diversion triggers automatic alerts

Public Transparency Requirements
Non‑sensitive allocation data must be published

Dashboards must reflect real‑time distribution metrics

5. Field Operations Standards
Non‑Punitive Engagement
No coercion, threats, or force

Civilians must be treated with dignity

Support must be voluntary whenever possible

Trauma‑Preventive Conduct
Use calm, stabilizing communication

Avoid escalation behaviors

Provide grounding support when needed

Documentation Integrity
All actions must be logged in real time

Missing or altered entries trigger accountability review

6. Telemetry Integration
Telemetry supports emergency logistics by:

Detecting infrastructure risks

Identifying resource shortages

Monitoring civilian safety indicators

Predicting system failures

Triggering early‑warning alerts

Telemetry ensures proactive logistics, not reactive crisis response.

7. Civilian Protection Role
Emergency logistics protects civilians by:

Ensuring access to essential resources

Preventing infrastructure‑related harm

Supporting safe evacuation or sheltering

Maintaining continuity of critical services

Prioritizing vulnerable populations

MOSD ensures that emergencies never become opportunities for coercion or harm.
