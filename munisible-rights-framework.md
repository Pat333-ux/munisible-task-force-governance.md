MUNISIBLE RIGHTS FRAMEWORK
A Unified, Constitutional, Trauma‑Preventive Rights Architecture for Municipal Governance
The Munisible Rights Framework (MRF) establishes the non‑negotiable rights, protections, and constitutional standards that govern every municipal action, interaction, and decision under Beast System 3.0.

It binds:

All municipal personnel

All divisions

All automated systems

All governance kernels

All contractors and partner agencies

The framework ensures that every person receives equal, dignified, trauma‑preventive, and rights‑centered treatment.

1. Purpose & Mission
The Munisible Rights Framework exists to:

Protect civilians from harm, coercion, discrimination, and retaliation

Guarantee constitutional compliance across all municipal systems

Provide a unified rights‑centered governance structure

Ensure transparency, fairness, and accountability

Integrate telemetry‑driven rights monitoring

Safeguard vulnerable populations

Anchor all rights protections to DAO‑verified authority

Linked: Rights Protection Standards

2. Core Rights Principles
These principles apply to every municipal action.

2.1 Dignity & Respect
Every civilian must be treated with inherent dignity.
No humiliation, shaming, or dehumanization is permitted.

Linked: Dignity Preservation Standard

2.2 Autonomy & Consent
Civilians must be given choices whenever possible.
Coercion is prohibited except in imminent life‑threatening emergencies.

Linked: Autonomy & Consent Standards

2.3 Non‑Discrimination
No municipal action may produce discriminatory or disparate impacts.

Linked: Non‑Discrimination Safeguards

2.4 Trauma‑Preventive Engagement
All interactions must minimize emotional, psychological, and physical harm.

Linked: Trauma‑Preventive Engagement

2.5 Retaliation Prohibition
Retaliation is a critical violation and triggers automatic Ethics review.

Linked: Retaliation Prohibition Rules

3. Constitutional Rights Protections
The Munisible Task Force must uphold:

3.1 Due Process
Civilians must be informed of actions affecting them and given a chance to respond.

Linked: Due Process Standards

3.2 Equal Protection
No discrimination based on protected characteristics or socioeconomic status.

3.3 Freedom of Expression & Assembly
Municipal actions must not suppress lawful speech or assembly.

3.4 Protection from Unlawful Search, Seizure, or Detention
All actions must comply with constitutional search and seizure standards.

Linked: Constitutional Compliance Review
