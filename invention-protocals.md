INVENTION PROTOCOLS
The Invention Protocols establish the mandatory workflow for documenting, validating, safeguarding, and integrating new inventions into the Beast System 3.0 ecosystem.
These protocols protect inventors, ensure ethical deployment, and maintain structural alignment with governance kernels.

Inventions may include:

Technical systems

Algorithms

Governance frameworks

Cultural or educational innovations

Operational tools

Experimental prototypes

All inventions must pass through the Sovereign Innovations Division.

1. Mission & Purpose
The Invention Protocols exist to:

Protect inventors’ rights and intellectual sovereignty

Ensure ethical and constitutional alignment of all innovations

Provide a standardized lifecycle for invention registration and review

Prevent misuse, exploitation, or unauthorized deployment

Anchor inventions to DAO governance and audit‑ready documentation

Support safe integration into municipal or organizational systems

These protocols ensure that innovation strengthens—not destabilizes—the Beast System 3.0 architecture.

2. Invention Lifecycle Overview
2.1 Invention Registration
Inventor submits a formal registration packet including description, purpose, diagrams, and intended use.

2.2 Identity & Ownership Verification
Identity Kernel verifies the inventor and anchors ownership to the DAO ledger.

2.3 Ethical & Rights Review
Ethics Kernel evaluates the invention for:

Dignity preservation

Harm prevention

Rights impact

Constitutional alignment

2.4 Risk & Telemetry Assessment
Telemetry Kernel evaluates:

Failure risk

Systemic impact

Pattern‑of‑practice implications

Vulnerability or misuse potential

2.5 Governance Integration Review
Civic Integrity Division ensures structural alignment with Beast System 3.0.

2.6 Approval, Conditional Approval, or Halt
Final determination:

APPROVED — Safe for integration

CONDITIONAL — Requires modifications

HALT — Violates ethical or constitutional standards
