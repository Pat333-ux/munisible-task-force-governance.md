1. Purpose of the Division Handbook
The Division Operations Handbook exists to:

Provide a unified governance framework for all divisions

Ensure consistent, ethical, rights‑centered operations

Define cross‑division responsibilities and boundaries

Maintain structural alignment with Beast System 3.0

Support transparent, stable, and constitutional governance

Every division must operate in full compliance with this handbook.
