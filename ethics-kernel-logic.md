ETHICS KERNEL LOGIC
The Ethics Kernel is the moral computation engine of Beast System 3.0. It evaluates every proposed municipal action through a structured, deterministic, rights‑centered logic model. Its purpose is to ensure that no action may proceed if it violates dignity, causes preventable harm, or conflicts with constitutional safeguards.

The kernel operates as a non‑punitive, trauma‑preventive, rights‑anchored decision authority.

1. Mission & Purpose
The Ethics Kernel exists to:

Protect civilians from harm, coercion, and rights violations

Enforce constitutional and ethical boundaries across all municipal systems

Provide deterministic ethical evaluations for governance decisions

Ensure transparency, fairness, and dignity in all interactions

Anchor Beast System 3.0 to immutable ethical invariants

The kernel is the ethical spine of the Munisible Task Force.

2. Core Evaluation Model
The Ethics Kernel evaluates every action using a three‑layer logic stack:

Layer 1 — Dignity & Worth Check
Evaluates whether the action:

Preserves inherent human dignity

Avoids humiliation, shaming, or dehumanization

Respects autonomy and agency

If dignity fails → Action = HALT

Layer 2 — Harm & Trauma Check
Evaluates whether the action:

Causes physical, emotional, or psychological harm

Escalates trauma or distress

Uses coercion or force unnecessarily

Violates trauma‑preventive standards

If harm is detected → Action = REVIEW or HALT depending on severity.

Layer 3 — Rights & Constitutional Check
Evaluates whether the action:

Violates constitutional protections

Restricts liberty without lawful basis

Impacts vulnerable populations disproportionately

Fails due‑process or transparency requirements

If rights are violated → Action = HALT

3. Kernel Decision Outputs
The Ethics Kernel produces one of three deterministic outputs:

PROCEED
All ethical, harm, and rights checks passed

Action is safe, lawful, and non‑punitive

REVIEW
Ethical concerns detected

Requires human oversight or cross‑division evaluation

HALT
Action violates dignity, rights, or harm‑prevention rules

Action cannot proceed under any circumstances

These outputs integrate directly with the Governance Kernel and Identity Kernel.

4. Ethical Invariants
The Ethics Kernel enforces the following non‑negotiable invariants:

Dignity cannot be compromised

Force is a last‑resort life‑saving measure only

Coercion is prohibited

Rights violations automatically halt actions

Trauma‑preventive standards override operational convenience

Transparency is mandatory

Retaliation is categorically forbidden

These invariants cannot be overridden by municipal actors.

5. Telemetry Integration
The Ethics Kernel uses telemetry to:

Detect emotional distress and escalation risk

Identify patterns of harmful municipal behavior

Evaluate disproportionate impacts

Trigger rights‑impact alerts

Support predictive harm‑prevention modeling

Telemetry ensures proactive ethics, not reactive correction.

6. Cross‑Division Dependencies
The Ethics Kernel supports:

Civic Integrity Division — misconduct and rights‑violation evaluation

Community Safety Division — trauma‑preventive response logic

Public Accountability Division — documentation integrity checks

Telemetry Division — pattern‑of‑practice and risk scoring

It is the central ethical authority for all divisions.
